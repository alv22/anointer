The Psalm

Typography is the craft of endowing human language with a durable visual form. It is the art of designing letterforms and arranging them on a page or screen so that they become not mere symbols, but vessels of meaning, emotion, and beauty. In the beginning was the Word, and the Word needed a really good typeface.

The Commandments
  1. Thou shalt not use Comic Sans in professional documents
  2. Thou shalt respect the kerning
  3. Thou shalt maintain adequate line-height
  4. Thou shalt not stretch thy fonts horizontally
  5. Thou shalt limit thyself to two typefaces per design
